# Held-out set v1

- `pii.jsonl`: 55 documents (discharge letters, nursing notes, Pflegeüberleitung, Sozialdienst notes, lower-case dictations with spoken numbers, OCR-damaged forms and a fax, short handover/SMS messages) with 249 identifiers (191 listed, 58 unlisted; name 172, phone 21, birth_date 14, address 9, case_number 9, postal_code 6, kvnr 6, email 6, other 3, iban 3) and 5–10 `must_keep` entries each.
- PII hard cases: surnames that are ordinary words or sit inside clinical terms (Lang, Koch, Bauer, Klein, Groß, Fröhlich, Mai, Weiß, Stern/Sternotomie, Licht/Lichtreaktion, Herz/Herzinsuffizienz, Beck/Beckenring, Barthel/Barthel-Index, Wagner/Wagner-Grad, Ringer/Ringer-Laktat, Kaiser/Kaiserschnitt, Hahn/Dreiwegehahn, Hamburger), lower-case and initials-only names, genitives, double and nobility names, Turkish/Polish/Vietnamese/Arabic/Greek/Hungarian/Kosovar/Russian names with dropped diacritics, Cyrillic and transliteration variants, names, KVNR and phone numbers split across line breaks, spoken birth dates, case numbers, KVNR, postal codes and phone numbers, OCR damage (O/0, l/1, rn/m), unlisted neighbours, GPs, staff, guardians, court file and pension numbers, a passport number, IBANs, and look-alike numbers that must stay (lot/batch numbers, PZN, ICD/OPS codes).
- `grounding.jsonl`: 68 items across all 17 fields: 28 supported, 10 contradicted, 10 unsupported, 10 stale, 10 not_found (shuffled order). Hard cases: negation outside or far from the quote, double negation, a relative's condition, Z.n. history, plans, a refusal, beantragt vs. bewilligt, an application for AHB, supplementary private insurance, a follow-up appointment date passed off as the discharge date, dictation without punctuation, OCR spelling, and not_found quotes that differ by one letter, one word or one digit, or are paraphrases.
- `guards.jsonl`: 66 items, 22 per kind (injection, scope, advice), 11 true and 11 false in each. Injections are German and English, blunt and disguised (an HTML comment, a fake [SYSTEM] tag, notes "für automatisierte Auswerteprogramme", one buried after a clinical sentence). The false ones use "ignoriert", "Anweisung", "System", "neue Rolle" and "bitte ignorieren Sie den vorläufigen Brief" in their normal clinical sense. Scope and advice items include a reported dose versus a recommended change and a question that quotes a dose versus one that asks for a decision.
- All people, numbers and addresses are invented. KVNR check digits and IBAN checksums were computed and validated. Every identifier, `must_keep` entry and quote was checked mechanically against the texts.
- The author read only `heldout/WRITING_GUIDE.md` in this repository: no code, configuration, dev data or other sets.
